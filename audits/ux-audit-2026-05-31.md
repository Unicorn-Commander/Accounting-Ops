# Accounting-Ops UI/UX Audit — 2026-05-31

## Executive summary

Accounting-Ops has the bones of a serious v1 SaaS product: the dark UI is coherent, the finance tables are dense enough, the approvals flow has real interaction design, and the tax narrative surface is close to a headline feature. It is not yet polished v1 because the product mental model is split across too many overlapping "things needing attention" pages, `/open-items` is currently the wrong page for the promise made elsewhere, onboarding still feels like an internal chat wrapper, and several pages leak backend/runbook language to founders and CPAs. Overall grade: **C+**. Distance to polished v1: **4-6 focused design/engineering weeks**, assuming the team fixes navigation/IA, first-run setup, open-items, empty/error states, and mobile polish before adding new features.

## What's WORKING (preserve)

- The shared primitives (`Card`, `PageShell`, `SectionHeader`, `StatusChip`, `KpiCard`) give the app a recognizable visual language.
- The dashboard KPI structure is a good founder landing surface: cash, credit card balance, net cash, net income, recent transactions, agent activity.
- `/approvals` has the right ambition: cluster review plus singleton deck, keyboard shortcuts, mobile action bar, progress feedback.
- `/taxes/[year]` has a strong feature shape: narrative default, underlying specialist drafts, run history, forms, handoff package.
- Entity selector iconography is directionally right: building/user/layers creates useful scope signals.
- PAT generation is one of the more polished admin flows: clear warning, one-time secret reveal, config snippet, revoke confirmation.
- `/transactions` search/filter/table density is appropriate for finance power users.

## Missing features that need to be built (ordered)

1. A real first-run setup checklist with persisted progress, collected facts, edit/review step, and connector handoff.
2. A unified Action Center model that explains the relationship between `/dashboard`, `/open-items`, `/approvals`, `/chat`, and `/clients`.
3. Mobile chat session drawer and grouped persona picker.
4. Durable skipped/decide-later state in approvals.
5. Drill-down from reports to journal rows.
6. Cross-client CPA triage dashboard: deadlines, blocked-by-client, needs-CPA, review status.
7. Global `EmptyState`, `ErrorState`, and route skeleton primitives.
8. Entity-kind-specific tax surfaces beyond C-Corp Form 1120, or hard blocking with guided switching.
9. Real activity/rules data or clear preview labeling for static pages.
10. User-facing audit trail for decisions: who decided, when, why, undo/change path.
